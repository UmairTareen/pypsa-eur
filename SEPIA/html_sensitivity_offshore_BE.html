Sensitivity Scenario Considering Additional Capacity for Belgium in Northsea

This sensitivity scenario is based on the negaWatt BE scenario by considering additional offshore wind capacity for Belgium (8GW by 2040 & 8GW by 2050) in the North Sea. In addition to existing plannings of increasing Belgium offshore capacity to 8 GW, up to 16 GW of extra capacity is also considered in some scenarios of Energyville in the wider North Sea area. However, the current Federal Development Plan only includes 3.5 GW additional offshore capacity. The results show that even the allowed extra capacity from North Sea is 16GW, only half of it is exploited.




Acronyms: combined heat and power (CHP), thermal energy storage (TES), carbon capture and utilisation (CCU), carbon capture, utlisation and sequestration (CCUS), variable operational and maintenence (VOM), combined cycle gas turbine (CCGT).
